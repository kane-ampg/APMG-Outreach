## Decisions
- **Query the database, don't estimate.** Every number in the document came from a live PostgREST read with exact counts, and the document says so in its own footnotes ("Source: live APMG platform database (Supabase) queried 28 July 2026").
- **No click-through rate as a headline — anywhere.** Corporate link scanners and mail-provider prefetch inflate `attribution_click`; bot/operator filtering is real but imperfect. Clicks are framed as a *relative interest signal between sectors*, with a caveat box on page 1 and a pointer back to it on page 2.
- **Lead with the one enquiry, and strip its PII.** The enquiry is the strongest evidence in the project, so it gets its own panel — but with the person's email address and mobile number removed, because the PDF will be forwarded.
- **Write the deliverable outside the repo.** `Desktop\APMG\` rather than the working tree; no `.gitignore` edit needed and nothing to accidentally commit.
- **Follow the `dataviz` procedure literally** — form first, colour by job, and *run* `validate_palette.js` instead of reasoning about contrast. The failing ramp proved the point.
- **Hand-written HTML + headless Chromium instead of a chart library.** Gives exact A4 pagination, no external asset fetches, and inline SVG marks that survive print.
- **Review the render as an image, not as source.** Reading the page PNGs is what caught the blank third of page 1 and the undersized charts.
- **Do not soften the asks page.** The shared-password stand-in and the client's missing trust documents are stated plainly, with owners; re-toning was offered rather than assumed.
