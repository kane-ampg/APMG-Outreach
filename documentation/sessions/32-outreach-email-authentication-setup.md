## Decisions
- **Verify DNS rather than trust the status report.** Every claim — the initial state, and all four of Evan's fixes — was resolved against **two** independent resolvers (`8.8.8.8` and `1.1.1.1`). This is what caught both the missing DKIM and the dropped `rua=`.
- **Warm up on seeded replies-first sends, not on prospects.** Reputation is built on engagement (replies, contact-adds, not-spam), and at 10/day one complaint is a 10% complaint rate. Burning the first sends on strangers is wasted volume with downside attached.
- **Warm up through Gmail web, not through the app.** The app's campaign webhook is live and unauthenticated with no pacing, and a real send writes an `email_sent` ledger row to `portal_events` that would pollute reporting. Gmail web needs no admin, no OAuth and no n8n.
- **`-all` stays, `~all` withdrawn.** Correct only because DKIM now signs; DKIM alignment carries DMARC through forwarding where SPF cannot.
- **`p=none` is deliberate during warm-up** — which is exactly why the missing `rua=` matters. Tighten to `p=quarantine` only after a few weeks of clean aggregate reports.
- **DMARC reporting address should be a digest service, not a mailbox.** Aggregate reports are gzipped XML and unreadable by hand.
- **Stay Services Admin** (the recommendation) — every remaining task sits outside Super Admin, and holding Super Admin in a third party's tenancy during their federation cutover is unnecessary exposure. **The user overrode this** and chose to request full Admin Console plus DNS access; that's their relationship to manage.
- **Route DNS through Zac, not Evan, and don't take shared MFA-bound credentials.** Send exact record values for someone else to publish.
- **Front-run the OAuth grant ahead of the SSO cutover.** Already-issued refresh tokens survive federation; a grant not yet made can be blocked or awkward during one.
- **No separate Google Cloud project.** n8n's managed OAuth2 supersedes the whole hand-rolled OAuth-client plan, including the Internal-vs-External consent-screen trap.
- **From name must be a real person who handles replies** — not a brand, and never an invented persona (Spam Act accurate sender identification, consistent with how ABN and sender identity have been handled elsewhere in this build).
- **BIMI is out of scope.** Wrong mechanism for the stated goal, requires quarantine/reject (incompatible with the current `p=none`), and a ~USD $1,000–1,500/yr certificate — disproportionate at 40–50 emails/day.
